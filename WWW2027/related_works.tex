\section{Related Works}
\label{sec:related_works}
\begingroup
\setlength{\emergencystretch}{2em}
Video recommendation benefits from modality-specific graph representations, as in MMGCN~\cite{wei2019mmgcn}, and MLLM-generated descriptions, as in DWYS~\cite{denadai2025dwys}.
DFF~\cite{sun2026dff} further studies representations from frozen MLLMs and their fusion with item IDs.
Hypergraphs also support recommendation: DHCN~\cite{xia2021dhcn} connects items through sessions, and MHCR~\cite{lyu2025mhcr} combines multiple graph views with hypergraph contrastive learning.
HGVRec constructs a hypergraph within each video, with scenes as nodes and contiguous action segments as hyperedges.

Hypergraph message passing is well established by HGNN~\cite{feng2019hgnn} and the multiset aggregation framework of AllSet~\cite{chien2022allset}.
ED-HNN~\cite{wang2023edhnn} introduces receiver-dependent hyperedge messages, and ZEN~\cite{bae2025zen} unifies linearized forms of representative hypergraph networks.
ASHA follows the general pattern of aggregation between nodes and hyperedges, using action continuity to define overlapping groups of scenes and incorporating segment extent into aggregation and video readout.

Structured visual representations encode actions through participants and relations: Grounded Situation Recognition~\cite{pratt2020gsr} models entities in semantic roles, while Action Genome~\cite{ji2020actiongenome} represents actions through spatiotemporal scene graphs.
REAF uses extracted actor, target, tool, and place assignments to fuse entity features into action representations for recommendation, supplying role-aware scene features to ASHA.

\endgroup
