\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\begingroup
\setlength{\emergencystretch}{1em}
We presented HGVRec, a video representation learning framework that organizes MLLM-extracted semantics through explicit structure.
ASHA groups scenes into maximal contiguous action segments and aggregates their information through a hypergraph, while REAF encodes entity participation roles within each scene.
Experiments on MicroLens-100K and M$^3$L-10M show that HGVRec achieves the highest mean NDCG@10 and HR@10 among the five evaluated variants.
With shared graph inputs, adding ASHA improves NDCG@10 on both datasets, and adding REAF provides further gains when ASHA is present.
These findings support explicit role and action-segment modeling as a way to improve the recommendation utility of available MLLM outputs.
Future work will examine comparisons with chronological scene encoders and attention, stronger MLLM extractors, and device-level efficiency to assess the framework's broader applicability.
\par
\endgroup
